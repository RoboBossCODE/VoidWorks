Voidworks is an independent experimental platform. Features may change, be unavailable or be removed.

Use a unique password for your Voidworks account. Never enter your Microsoft password directly into Voidworks; Microsoft authentication must take place on Microsoft's own sign-in service.

Reasonable security measures should be used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Voidworks is not intended to operate as a secure vault, password manager, financial service, medical record system or confidential document store.

Accounts may later be suspended or restricted for abuse, spam, attempts to compromise the service, or serious breaches of community rules. Moderation features will be documented before public posting features are enabled.
