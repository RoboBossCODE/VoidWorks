GHOST//PROTOCOL // BRIEFING

YOUR MISSION.

Follow the connected nodes to the data vault. Select a discovered node, scan its traffic and reconstruct the scrambled four-symbol access key. Every scan and failed attempt increases your trace. Get to the vault and extract the data before your trace reaches 100%.

01SELECT

Tap a reachable node on the network map.

02SCAN

Reveal the packets on that node.

03DECODE

Reassemble its access key in sequence order.

04EXTRACT

Reach the final node and finish your mission.

This is a puzzle game with invented commands and networks. It never connects to real devices.
